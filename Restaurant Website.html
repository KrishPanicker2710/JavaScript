🌍 My Story — The Beginning of Cross-Worlds

Cross-Worlds started with something I’ve always loved: new food and learning about different places around the world.
Whenever I tried a dish from another country, I was amazed by how much more than flavour it could offer. A single meal could tell a story about a culture, a tradition, or a place I had never visited.
That gave me an idea:
"What if I could bring all those experiences together in one restaurant?"
That idea became "Cross-Worlds".
I imagined a place where you don't need a plane ticket to explore the world. You can sit at one table and travel from India to Italy, from Japan to Mexico, and from Korea to the Middle East—all through food.
I created Cross-Worlds to share that feeling of discovery with everyone. I want every dish on the menu to feel like a little passport stamp and every visit to be a new adventure.
For me, Cross-Worlds isn't just about serving food. "It's about curiosity, culture, and bringing people together through something everyone understands—great food."
So welcome to my little journey around the world.
"Take a seat, choose your destination, and let your taste buds do the travelling. ✈️🌎"
